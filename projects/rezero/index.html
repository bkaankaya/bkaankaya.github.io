01 / CONTEXT

The challenge

Responsive combat depends on simulation timing, input buffering, clear feedback and animation transitions.

02 / ENGINEERING

The approach

Created a JavaScript/HTML5 Canvas engine with a fixed-step loop, coyote time, jump buffering, particle effects and state-driven opponent logic.

03 / NOTES

Key details

  • Custom update loop, movement and combat state machines.
  • Timing mechanics such as hitstop and jump buffering.
  • Browser-based rendering with desktop experimentation.

Scope & verification

Fan-inspired software prototype; not an official product of the underlying franchise.
